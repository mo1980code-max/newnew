# Learn to Pray with Layan

A gentle English-language, 16:9 children's prayer tutorial in a polished 3D family-animation look.

> Note: the supplied brief contained detailed visual directions for scenes 1–6. Scenes 7–15 below complete the full sequence so the video has all 15 scenes in order. Prayer details can vary; children should learn with a trusted parent or teacher.

## Voice and visual continuity

- **Narrator:** Layan, warm and encouraging English feminine voice.
- **Character:** Layan is a cheerful seven-year-old Arab girl with hazel-brown eyes, rosy cheeks, a pastel-pink floral prayer dress, and a soft white hijab.
- **Setting:** a bright, cozy sunlit room with a colorful geometric prayer mat.
- **Format:** 1280 x 720, 16:9, 25 fps, English on-screen text.

## 15-scene script

| Scene | On-screen title | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **WELCOME** | Hello, friends! I’m Layan. Today we will learn how to pray, step by step, with calm hearts and beautiful manners. Let’s begin together. |
| 2 | **FACE THE QIBLAH** | First, make wudu and wear clean, modest clothes. Face the Qiblah, the direction of the Ka’bah, and make the intention in your heart for Allah. |
| 3 | **TAKBEER** | Stand straight, raise both hands to your shoulders or ears, and say, Allahu Akbar, meaning Allah is the Greatest. |
| 4 | **HANDS AND OPENING DUA** | Fold your right hand over your left on your chest. Quietly say the opening supplication, Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdik, wa tabarak asmuk, wa ta’ala jadduk, wa la ilaha ghayruk. |
| 5 | **RECITATION** | Recite Surah Al-Fatihah, then another short surah, such as Al-Ikhlas. Read calmly and listen to your heart. |
| 6 | **RUKU** | Say Allahu Akbar and bow into Ruku. Keep your back straight, place your hands on your knees, and say, Subhana Rabbiyal Adheem, three times. |
| 7 | **RISE FROM RUKU** | To rise from Ruku, we lift our heads and say, Sami Allahu liman hamidah. Once we are standing straight, we say, Rabbana wa lakal hamd. |
| 8 | **FIRST SUJOOD** | Then we say Allahu Akbar and go into the first Sujood. Seven parts touch the mat: the forehead, the nose, both palms, both knees, and the toes. We say, Subhana Rabbiyal A’la, three times. |
| 9 | **SIT BETWEEN PROSTRATIONS** | We raise our head while saying Allahu Akbar, sit calmly, and keep our back straight. We rest our hands near our knees and say, Rabbighfir li, Rabbighfir li. |
| 10 | **SECOND SUJOOD** | Then we make the second Sujood and say, Subhana Rabbiyal A’la, three times. Then we say Allahu Akbar and stand for the second rak’ah. |
| 11 | **SECOND RAK’AH** | We perform the second rak’ah just like the first: Al-Fatihah and a short surah, then Ruku, rising, and two prostrations. |
| 12 | **MIDDLE TASHAHHUD** | After the second prostration, we sit and gently point with the right index finger toward the Qiblah, reciting the middle Tashahhud: At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat. |
| 13 | **THIRD AND FOURTH RAKAH** | In three- and four-rak’ah prayers, we stand to complete the remaining rak’ahs. In these cycles, we recite only Surah Al-Fatihah quietly. |
| 14 | **FINAL TASHAHHUD AND DUA** | In the final sitting, we recite the complete Tashahhud and follow it with the Ibrahimic prayer: Allahumma salli ala Muhammad wa ala ali Muhammad. |
| 15 | **TASLEEM AND ENDING** | Then we turn to the right and say, Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullah. We turn to the left and repeat it. May Allah accept our prayer! |

## Production note

The generated MP4 uses one narrated audio chapter for every three scenes, with gentle scene changes, English title cards, and clean lower-third guidance. Scenes 7–15 use one consistent Layan character with corrected prayer poses and narration-derived timing. The first and second Sujood frames keep the forehead and nose visibly touching the mat.
