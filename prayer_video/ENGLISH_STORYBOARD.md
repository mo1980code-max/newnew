A gentle English-language, 16:9 children's prayer tutorial in a polished 3D family-animation look.

## 15-scene script

| Scene | On-screen title | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **WELCOME** | Hello, friends! I’m Layan. Today we will learn how to pray, step by step, with calm hearts and beautiful manners. Let’s begin together. |
| 2 | **FACE THE QIBLAH** | First, make wudu and wear clean, modest clothes. Face the Qiblah, the direction of the Ka’bah, and make the intention in your heart for Allah. |
| 3 | **TAKBEER** | Stand straight, raise both hands to your shoulders or ears, and say, Allahu Akbar, meaning Allah is the Greatest. |
| 4 | **HANDS AND OPENING DUA** | Fold your right hand over your left on your chest. Quietly say the opening supplication, Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdik, wa tabarak asmuk, wa ta’ala jadduk, wa la ilaha ghayruk. |
| 5 | **RECITATION** | Recite Surah Al-Fatihah, then another short surah, such as Al-Ikhlas. Read calmly and listen to your heart. |
| 6 | **RUKU** | Say Allahu Akbar and bow into Ruku. Keep your back straight, place your hands on your knees, and say, Subhana Rabbiyal Adheem, three times. |
| 7 | **RISE FROM RUKU** | Rise up slowly while saying, Sami Allahu liman hamidah. Then stand straight and say, Rabbana wa lakal hamd. |
| 8 | **FIRST SUJOOD** | Say Allahu Akbar and go down into Sujood. Place your forehead and nose, both hands, knees, and toes on the mat. Say, Subhana Rabbiyal A’la, three times. |
| 9 | **SIT BETWEEN PROSTRATIONS** | Say Allahu Akbar and sit calmly between the two prostrations. Keep your back straight and say, Rabbighfir li, meaning, My Lord, forgive me. |
| 10 | **SECOND SUJOOD** | Say Allahu Akbar and make the second Sujood. Stay humble and repeat, Subhana Rabbiyal A’la, three times. |
| 11 | **SECOND RAK’AH** | Say Allahu Akbar and stand for the second rak’ah. Repeat the standing, recitation, Ruku, rising, and two prostrations. |
| 12 | **TASHAHHUD** | After the second rak’ah, sit for Tashahhud. Rest your hands on your thighs and raise your right index finger gently as you recite. |
| 13 | **BLESSINGS AND DUA** | Send blessings upon Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, and make a sincere dua for goodness. Sit quietly and remember Allah. |
| 14 | **SALAM** | Finish the prayer with Salam. Turn your head to the right and say, Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullah. Then turn left and repeat it. |
| 15 | **WELL DONE** | Well done! Prayer is a beautiful conversation with Allah. Practice with a parent or teacher, take your time, and keep your heart gentle. See you next time! |

The generated MP4 uses one narrated audio chapter for every three scenes, with gentle scene changes, English title cards, and clean lower-third guidance. Arabic prayer phrases are preserved in transliteration so the English narration remains respectful and usable as a teaching guide.
